**Acceptable Use**

Hubs gives you the freedom to create amazing spaces and share them with people across the globe. That being said there are certain things that are not permitted:

* Break any applicable law or do anything illegal.

* Harass, threaten or violate the privacy of others. 

* Harvest or collect personal information without the explicit consent. 

* Send unsolicited communications, monitor, intercept or modify communications not meant for you.

* Engage in an activity that interferes or impedes the Hubs instance, services, servers or networks associated with the Hubs instance.

* Transmit, display, download, upload or otherwise grant access to content that includes graphic depictions of violence or sexuality.

* Use any malware or malicious code such as spyware, viruses, time bombs, trojan horses or worms on the platform.

* Violate the intellectual property, copyright patent, or trademark rights of others.

* Defraud, deceive, commit identity theft, phish, exploit, harm or mislead others.

* Violate the privacy of others.

* Engage in any kind of hate speech, harass, dox, bully, threaten, insult, intimidate, degrade, promote prejudicial action, incite violence against a person based on religion, race, age, disability, geographic location, gender, sexual orientation, national origin or other protected category.

* Engage in solicitation.

* Buy, sell or advertise controlled or illegal services or products.

* Promote or engage in illegal gambling

These are only examples. Do not consider this a complete list as we may update it from time to time. If you are considering an action that you would not like done to yourself please refrain from doing so.
